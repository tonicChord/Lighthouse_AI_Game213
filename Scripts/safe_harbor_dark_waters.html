Dark Waters

  • Left-Click (Hold): Illuminate the darkness. Scroll Wheel adjusts beam size.
  • Strict Rule of Light: Cargo ships only sail forward when illuminated.
  • Rule of Darkness: Unlit ships are immediately blown sideways by the storm into deadly rocks!
  • Lightning Flashes: Sporadic bursts of lightning will reveal the map. Use them to locate ships without spending battery.
  • Combat: Right-click fires a Cannon at Pirates. Shine your light on Krakens to repel them.
  • Scoring: Guide ships to the massive Safe Harbor (bottom right). Smallest light usage = highest Eco-multiplier.
